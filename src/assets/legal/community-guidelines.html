Amor Community Guidelines

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Adults Only

Amor is an 18+ dating community. Under-18 users are not allowed.

Objectionable Content

Do not post or send content involving minors, nudity, pornography, sexual exploitation, sexual solicitation, harassment, hate, threats, graphic violence, scams, spam, malware or illegal activity.

Profiles

  • Use real, accurate profile information.
  • Do not impersonate others or misrepresent your identity.
  • Do not upload explicit, violent, hateful or deceptive images.

Messages

  • Respect consent and boundaries.
  • Do not pressure, threaten, harass or sexually coerce anyone.
  • Do not send spam, scams, payment requests or suspicious links.

Reporting and Blocking

Use report and block tools on profiles and conversations when content or behavior feels unsafe. Amor reviews reports and may remove content, warn users, restrict accounts or ban accounts.

Appeals and Support

For moderation questions or appeal requests, contact support@amor.app.
