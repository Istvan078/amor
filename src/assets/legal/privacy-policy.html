Amor Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Who Can Use Amor

Amor is only for adults who are at least 18 years old. Do not create an account or use Amor if you are under 18.

Data We Collect

  • Account data, including email address, authentication identifiers and account status.
  • Profile data, including name, birth date, age, gender, sexual orientation, relationship goals, preferences and profile text.
  • Photos and verification images, including profile pictures and verification selfies.
  • Location data used for matching distance and local recommendations.
  • Messages, GIF selections, read state, reactions and conversation metadata.
  • Safety data, including reports, blocks, warnings, moderation audit records and risk signals.
  • Device and notification data, including push tokens and platform identifiers.
  • Purchase state, subscription status and entitlement information processed through RevenueCat and app store billing providers.
  • Optional analytics and crash data if you consent to those uses.

How We Use Data

We use data to operate accounts, build profiles, match users, deliver messages, process subscriptions, detect abuse, moderate content, provide support, improve the app and meet legal or safety obligations.

Third-Party Services

Amor uses Firebase for authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions, push notifications and related infrastructure. Amor uses RevenueCat for subscription and purchase entitlement processing. These providers process data only as needed to provide their services to Amor.

Location Disclosure

Amor asks for location permission to show nearby recommendations and distance-based discovery. You can deny permission and provide location manually where supported.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with infrastructure providers, moderation/support tooling, legal authorities when required, and other users as part of profile, discovery and messaging features you use.

Retention and Deletion

You can request account deletion in the app. Account deletion removes the account profile, profile images, verification selfies, match indexes, incoming likes, conversations and authentication account where technically possible. Reports, moderation audit records and safety evidence may be retained for abuse prevention, legal compliance, dispute handling or safety review.

Your Choices

You can manage privacy consent, optional analytics, personalization and marketing choices in the app. You can also contact support for access or deletion requests.

Contact

For privacy, safety or account questions, contact support@amor.app.
